1.1 Agentic AI la gi va tai sao quan trong?
Agentic AI la the he moi cua tri tue nhan tao, khong chi tra loi cau hoi ma con tu dong thuc hien ca
mot quy trinh lam viec nhieu buoc voi kha nang lap ke hoach, ra quyet dinh va hanh dong doc lap.
Khac voi chatbot hay RPA truyen thong theo quy tac co dinh, AI Agent co the: (1) Phan tich boi
canh; (2) Phan ra nhiem vu con; (3) Tuong tac voi he thong ben ngoai; (4) Dieu chinh khi co tinh
huong bat ngo; (5) Hoan thanh muc tieu cuoi cung ma khong can con nguoi can thiep lien tuc.
Theo McKinsey (2025), Agentic AI la 'nuoc di chien luoc tiep theo trong tu dong hoa doanh nghiep'
- tu cong cu ho tro workflow, nay AI co the so huu toan bo workflow. Do la nen tang de mot startup
cong nghe co the tao ra san pham co gia tri cao cho SME.

2.1 NOI DAU DOANH NGHIEP (Pain Points)
Nhom 1: Nhap lieu & Xu ly chung tu thu cong
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• 66% doi ngu ke toan AP van nhap thu cong du lieu hoa don vao he thong ERP (IFOL
2025)
• 63% doi ngu mat hon 10 gio/tuan chi xu ly hoa don; trung binh 15 gio nhap lieu + 12 gio
doi soat
• Ty le loi nhap lieu thu cong: 10-15%; moi loi mat trung binh $53 de sua + rui ro phat
tuan thu
• Quy trinh: Nhan hoa don (email/giay) -> In ra -> Nhap tay vao Excel/ERP -> Doi soat ->
Gui duyet -> Thanh toan -> Luu tru
• Thoi gian xu ly 1 hoa don thu cong: trung binh 19.5 ngay; chi phi $13.11/hoa don
(PLANERGY 2025)

Nhom 2: Doi soat tai khoan & Ket so sach
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• Doi soat ngan hang: Kiem tra thu cong tung dong tren bao cao ngan hang vs so sach noi
bo; mat 2-4 ngay/thang
• Ket so sach cuoi thang/quy: Doi ngu lam viec thu cong qua dem; sai so giua cac he
thong (CRM, ERP, ngan hang) gay cham tre
• Nhieu cong ty ghi nhan ty le sai sot 3-5% tren cac giao dich doi soat; chi phi sua sai leo
thang theo quy mo
• Khong co kha nang real-time: phat hien van de chi o ket thuc ky ke toan, khong phai luc
phat sinh
• 40-60% thoi gian nhan su ke toan di vao cong viec gia tri thap (nhap lieu, doi soat) thay
vi phan tich chien luoc

III. SAN XUAT
(MANUFACTURING)

Smart Factory & Industrial AI
Automation

Linh vuc san xuat dang trong cuoc cach mang Industry 4.0 lan 2 - tu tu dong hoa vat ly (robot,
PLC) sang tu dong hoa tri tue (AI Agent dieu phoi toan bo qua trinh san xuat). McKinsey (2025)
bao cao nha may ap dung Agentic AI dat giam >20% chi phi logistics & ton kho va cai thien dang
ke ty le phat hien loi san pham.

3.1 NOI DAU DOANH NGHIEP (Pain Points)
Nhom 1: Ke hoach san xuat & Quan ly don hang
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• Lap ke hoach san xuat: Thu thap don hang tu nhieu kenh (email, ERP, Excel) -> Ket hop
voi ton kho nguyen lieu -> Lap lich tay trong Excel; mat 1-2 ngay/tuan
• Khi co thay doi don hang dot xuat (khach huy/them), lich san xuat phai sua tay toan bo;
gay ton thoi gian va sai sot
• Kho toi uu: Thieu cong cu tinh toan opt. combination giua cong suat may, nguon luc
nhan su va deadline giao hang
• Ton kho nguyen lieu khong dong bo voi ke hoach san xuat: Thuong xuyen thieu nguyen
lieu hoac du thua gay dong von
• Bao cao hieu suat san xuat (OEE, takt time) phu thuoc nhap lieu thu cong; so lieu tre 1-3
ngay so voi thuc te

Nhom 2: Kiem soat chat luong (QC)
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• Kiem tra chat luong bang mat thuong: 2-4 nhan vien QC kieu tra thu cong; ty le phat
hien loi chi dat 70-80%; moi mat mot nhan vien co kinh nghiem mat 6-12 thang dao tao
• Ghi nhan loi san pham: Dien form giay hoac Excel theo ca lam; kho truy vet nguon goc
loi (may nao, ca nao, lo nguyen lieu nao)
• Xu ly san pham loi: Phat hien tre (cuoi chuyen), luc do san pham loi da tich luy nhieu; chi
phi tai che cao
• Khong co he thong du bao loi: Thieu kha nang phan tich du lieu lich su de canh bao xu
huong loi truoc khi xay ra

Nhom 3: Bao tri thiet bi & Downtime
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• Bao tri theo lich co dinh (calendar-based): Thay the linh kien theo dinh ky du thiet bi van
tot; lang phi chi phi bao tri

4.1 NOI DAU DOANH NGHIEP (Pain Points)
Nhom 1: Lap ke hoach van chuyen & Tuyen duong
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• Lap lich van chuyen hang ngay: Thu thap don hang -> Phan cong xe/tai xe thu cong ->
In phieu -> Giao ca -> Theo doi bang dien thoai
• Toi uu tuyen duong (Route Optimization): Chay bang kinh nghiem tai xe hoac Google
Maps thu cong; khong co bai toan VRP (Vehicle Routing Problem) tu dong
• Khi co thay doi don hang dot xuat (huy/them/doi dia chi), dieu phoi vien phai lien lac lai,
chinh lich tay; gay delay
• Khong theo doi real-time: Quan ly khong biet chinh xac xe dang o dau, ETA cho khach
hang la 'uoc luong'
• Bao cao hieu suat tai xe & xe: Tong hop thu cong cuoi tuan; kho phat hien van de som

Nhom 2: Quan ly kho & Kiem ke
Dac diem quy trinh hien tai
• Nhap/xuat kho: Quet barcode thu cong hoac ghi so tay; sai sot nhap lieu dan den so lieu
kho khong chinh xac
• Kiem ke hang ton: Dem tay dinh ky (tuan/thang/quy); mat 1-3 ngay; dan doan xuat
nhap; phat hien chenh lech tre
• Slotting kho (bo tri hang): Sap xep theo cam nhan; hang ban chay dat o vi tri kho toi uu;
tang thoi gian picking
• Xu ly tra hang (reverse logistics): Nhap hang tra tay, kiem tra, phan loai, cap nhat he
thong; quy trinh cham va mat nhieu nhan luc
• Khong co visibility xuyen kho: Cong ty nhieu kho khong biet ton kho tong the theo
real-time
